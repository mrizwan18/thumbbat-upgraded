ThumbBat must feel like a premium colorful competitive mobile game, not a dark cinematic/esports interface.

Prioritize colorful cartoon readability, exaggerated silhouettes, chunky UI, playful animation, strong visual hierarchy and energetic feedback.

Avoid:

predominantly black/dark screens
flat generic UI
thin typography
realistic/photorealistic rendering
excessive neon
sterile minimalism
unnecessary 3D complexity

The visual target is premium cartoon mobile-game energy, inspired by the readability and playfulness of games such as Clash Royale and Ludo, while maintaining a distinct ThumbBat identity.
